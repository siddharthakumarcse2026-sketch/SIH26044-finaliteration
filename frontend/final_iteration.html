The Problem We Solve

🎓

Skill Gap

Students acquire skills in academia that often don't match industry expectations, leaving them unprepared for real-world roles.

🏢

Industry Struggle

Companies face challenges finding candidates with the right competencies, leading to longer hiring cycles and mismatched placements.

👨‍🏫

Limited Visibility

Academicians lack access to industry internship opportunities and current practices, making curriculum alignment difficult.

🔌

Fragmented Ecosystem

No unified platform exists connecting students, universities, and industries for collaboration, mentorship, and placements.
